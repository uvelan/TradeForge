## Verified behavior and boundaries

- Replay checks source-order uniqueness and the bid/ask side, preserves a valid early stop/target, reports later quote holes and incomplete time-exit windows as unresolved, and withholds results on incomplete coverage. All remain regression requirements.
- A single cataloged tick partition, one simulated position and fixed notional-bps costs are intentional prototype constraints. No order routing, hosted services, exchange-only corporate actions for initial spot FX, or enterprise infrastructure is required.
- Same-feed multi-partition **candle** SMT, correction-linked alert exclusion, and browser-independent polling with a broker double exist. Ordinary invalidation, restart ownership and missed intervals are separate issues.
- Duplicate ingest, checksum read failure, time cutoffs, cross-origin refusal and exception-before-publication rollback already have coverage in at least one catalog path. Hard process termination and research-import corrupt duplicate are distinct cases.
