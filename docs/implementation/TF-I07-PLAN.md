# TF-I07 plan

Base 3b3e01a9c66c4df4164b28d91d12c2da967180bd, the approved TF-I06 merge.

CRT baseline v1 uses a closed HTF candle's first open, maximum high, minimum low and last close over every expected observation, with midpoint (high + low)/2. Reuse canonical coverage and history verification. UTC H1/H4/D1 and IANA local daily boundaries are supported with aligned observations. Native evidence uses the same canonical reader and UTC boundaries, and must match complete constituent evidence before release. Client and archive authority remain distinct. Native requests share a combined bound of 10,000 slots, 30,000 decoded rows and 50 partitions. Reuse matching archive reads within each request.

At max(period end, constituent availability, native availability), a positive-width reference activates. Require subsequent LTF candle start at or after availability to exclude straddling candles. Latest completed references coexist within a configurable cap, with explicit replacement at the cap. A fixed reference start optionally disables rolling selection. Penetration must strictly exceed the boundary and meet its inclusive absolute minimum. Reclaim must close strictly inside and meet its inclusive minimum distance. Both-boundary sweeps are terminal ambiguity. A failed same-candle reclaim is a candidate, expires in accepted observations and never confirms retrospectively. Later observations can record midpoint and opposite boundary, with no intrabar sequence inferred. Expiry, continuity gaps, a new extreme or configured invalidation produce separate terminal evidence.

Hash market, exact constituent/native evidence and rules separately from request snapshot, and chain later events with exact observation evidence. Use bounded independent EventPages, current directly from live book, and share a capped chart primitive. Optional same-source contextual references are informational and strictly availability gated. Validate exact numerical / DST / archive / delayed evidence, rolling IDs, paging, and browser rewind coexistence before review. Targets on the Windows fixture host: CRT P95 under 600 ms on 2,000 bars, and combined indicators P95 under 3 s on 2,000 bars and 8 s for an extended saved request.
